## Notes

- **JinxDiceImp**: Flies: the die hovers 0.4-1 px over the feet point (T1 limit 16 px including the hover).
- **JinxRoulette**: New T3 (replaces JinxRattler). The body crawls through its own mouth: Idle moves the scales 7.5 deg a frame (one pocket per loop), Run 10 deg a frame (two pockets per loop), so both loops repeat without a jump.
- **JinxGrandJester**: Idle/Run wear COMEDY, IdleSad/RunSad wear TRAGEDY; Juggle is the comedy basic, Bash the tragedy basic; Flip goes comedy -> tragedy, FlipBack tragedy -> comedy.
- **JinxHighRoller**: FxJinxStake P0..P6 (12 x 12, origin (6, 10)) floats over the fedora and shows the stake.
- **JinxJackBox**: Wind (4) is the idle shown while it winds itself up (Wound Up); the music-note count is FxJinxWind.
- **JinxCardSharp**: Ace is the third Deal: the gold Ace card (FxJinxAce) instead of three cards.
- **JinxMarionette**: MirrorIdle/MirrorRun: the stance while it mirrors the hero (strings glowing, head tilted).
- **JinxFortuneTeller**: Idle/Run/Foretell show a clouded ball; the Tower (red), Fool (gold) and Star (blue) sets show the coming fate.
- **JinxSnakeEyes**: Dazed (4) loops while it has crapped out.
- **JinxCardSoldier**: EdgeIdle/EdgeRun: turned edge-on (Paper Thin), a 2 px card.
- **JinxCardKing**: CourtIdle/CourtRun: the stance while Hold Court is up (card raised, crown glowing).
- **JinxHouseOfCards**: Idle/Run/Topple/Storm = 3 tiers; Idle2/Run2/Topple2/Storm2 and Idle1/Run1/Topple1/Storm1 after each lost tier; Whirl/WhirlRun/WhirlSlash/Storm0 at 0 tiers (Storm0 casts on frame 3).
- **JinxTwenty**: Fumble (4) loops while a natural 1 has it dazed.
- **JinxNutcracker**: IdleSlow/RunSlow: the key running down (the tell); Rewind loops while it is frozen rewinding.
- **JinxOracle**: FxJinxOracleGhost Act: same cell/feet as JinxOracle; its fan looses on frame 4 at 0.52 s, exactly 1.0 s before her own (Prophecy frame 9 at 1.52 s after the ghost spawns on frame 2).
- **JinxBandit**: Jam (4) loops while it has been tilted (three skulls).
- **JinxChimera**: IdleBurn/RunBurn: the mane alight (Last Act, below half health).
- **FxJinxBetWheel**: Roulette Place Your Bets: 74 x 34, origin (37, 17) = the centre of the bets (rx 34, ry 15, the inner quarter empty). Wedge i (0..7) is centred at angle i*45-90 deg; even = red, odd = black.
- **FxJinxWedgeBurst**: One erupting wedge (Red / Black / Zero), origin = the wedge centre on the floor; damage frames 1-2.
- **FxJinxPot**: High Roller's pot: Land, Tick (loops while it ticks), Burst (pays out), Bust (hit while down).
- **FxJinxWind**: Jack-in-the-Box Wound Up indicator One..Three (head overlay).
- **FxJinxAce**: Fly: the Card Sharp's Ace projectile; Over: the head overlay while the next deal is the Ace.
- **FxJinxLucky**: Dice Imp Lucky Six head overlay.
- **FxJinxStrung**: Puppeteer: Bead (laid along each string), Cross (head overlay on each strung ally).
- **FxHoop**: Fly: the Ringmaster's hoop; Encore: the white-gold hoop of an encore.
- **FxJinxEyeOrb**: Fly: the Oracle's orb; Ghost: the harmless omen orbs her prophecy ghost looses.
- Death plays once and ends on the corpse; Dead is that corpse frame. Statue = the Idle frame mapped tone-for-tone
