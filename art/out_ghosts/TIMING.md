# Ghost forms + blob fodder: sprite timing

Generated by `tools/sprites/ghost_rigs.py`. Frame indices are 0-based within the anim.
Monster sheets use feet = (cellW/2, cellH-2) and face right; FX use the fx.py centre point listed below.
Times are seconds from the start of the anim to the start of that frame.
All six blobs are ONE ectoplasm (drawn by ghosts_v2_blobs.py): a lit gel skin over a darker core, a backlit rim,
glowing hearts, bubbles and what each has swallowed. Mote one big eye; Spitter a goo sac and a nozzle; Gel one
heart; Brute two hearts, rocks and a bone, fists; Titan three hearts under a crust of bone plates; King a gold
crown, an ermine-collared cape and the skulls of its subjects.

Design scale: the kits' offsets are authored in design units that ArtScale.cs multiplies by the sheet's factor
(SCALE below); the art is drawn at its final size. Blob cells are locked (code depends on them).
Brute, Titan and King grow with each fusion: GhostPack multiplies their kit offsets, hitboxes and node by GROW
on top of the design scale.

## Ghost forms in game (tools/sprites/ghost_forms.py)

In-game bodies of the Gleaner, Binder and Poltergeist (the Wisp keeps the vanilla ghost). 54 x 50 cells = the
vanilla ghost's own cell (AtlasEnemies ghost_p1_blue_*), drawn at the same place in it, so the cloned ghost prefab
keeps its sprite anchor (BotCenter, offset (-1, -7.5)), HitZone and attack node. Origin (the ghost's transform) =
cell pixel (28, 42); sprite coords below are relative to it (x right, y up NEGATIVE).

Player colours work exactly like the vanilla ghost: shroud/hair = the 4 vanilla shroud colours, head/hands = the 4
vanilla skin yellows, eyes = the vanilla eye pink, all at alpha 158; GhostPack gives each form clip an atlas whose
material is a copy of that player's ghost material (P1 SpriteTinted, P2-P4 SpritePaletted + their ramp), so P2-P4
remap those colours to orange / green / tan like the vanilla ghost. Props (sack, chains, cutlery, hex fire) use
colours every ramp leaves alone (ghost_forms.check_ramps asserts both).
