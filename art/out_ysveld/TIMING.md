## Notes

- **YsvRimeling**: IdleSnap / RunSnap: the fangs burn white while the hero carries 2 frost pips (Cold Snap).
- **YsvHoarmoth**: Flies 6 px over its shadow; the T1 limit (20 x 16) includes the hover.
- **YsvSilkmoth**: Flies 10 px over its shadow; the trailing cocoon silk sways with the idle and run.
- **YsvLurker**: The frost lid hides all but its red eyes while it waits (Idle); Run lifts it a crack.
- **YsvMatron**: IdleWrap / RunWrap: the hourglass glows red while her next Impale is doubled (Wrap).
- **YsvTarantula**: Idle/Run carry 3 Broodlings in the fur, Idle2/Run2 two, Idle1/Run1 one, Idle0/Run0 none.
- **YsvGlasswing**: Curled loops inside the cocoon; Stunned loops when it is cracked open; IdleKeen / RunKeen: the scythes burn cyan while her next slash is doubled.
- **YsvBride**: IdleWidow / RunWidow / BreathWidow / SweepWidow: the same moves with the veil turned black (Widowed).
- **YsvColossus**: IdleIce / RunIce: it leans forward and frost streams off it while it stands on ice (Downhill).
- **YsvGeode**: IdleSeam / RunSeam: the seam glows from 60 % health. Split: it cracks in two. Half*: the half it is after the split (YsvGeodeHalf is the other half, a separate monster).
- Death plays once and ends on the corpse; Dead is that corpse frame. Statue = the Idle frame mapped tone-for-tone
  onto the stone ramp. Every attack has at least two wind-up frames before its first event.
