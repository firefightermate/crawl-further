## Notes

- **DeityGoldIdolSylvene**: The gold idol's portrait: the pod opens and closes once per loop (closed, splitting, open in flower).
- **FxSeed**: Life lasts 2.5 s (the ripening), then Ripe loops; FxSylSeedFast is the Gall Wasp's striped seed (Life 1.5 s). Shake is the Spore Husk's tell (pods shake for a beat before they burst).
- **FxSylBush**: Drag/Taut: the vine runs along +x to the Behemoth (the code turns the bush to face it); FxSylBushSwing sweeps the arc behind the Behemoth (origin at its feet).
- **FxSylDroneRing**: Rolls out to rx 50 / ry 25 in 0.42 s; the code dazes a hero the ring's edge crosses.
- **SylBriarChampion**: Idle/Run/Thrust/Lunge carry the lance at its base length (18 px); Idle1-3, Run1-3, Thrust1-3 and Lunge1-3 at +6, +12, +18 px with one fresh green tip per grown length.
- **SylCarrionLily**: Idle/Run: uprooted, walking on its roots; IdlePlanted: roots dug in (Planted); Plant is the 3-frame dig.
- **SylCarrionQueen**: IdleBlur/RunBlur: the wings blur for the 0.3 s before each drone ring (the tell).
- **SylRotfly**: Flies: hovers 4 px over the feet point (counted in the 16 px T1 height).
- **SylShambler**: Death is the burst: the corpse swells and splits into three seeds (spawned by the code on frame 3).
- **SylSower**: New T3 (replaces SylAmberHornet). The drill ribs turn half a rib per idle frame, one per run frame.
- **SylStrangler**: Lasso is the special when a seed or pod lies within 50 px; Lash replaces it when none does.
- Death plays once and ends on the corpse; Dead is that corpse frame. Statue = the Idle frame mapped tone-for-tone
  onto the stone ramp. Every attack has at least two wind-up frames before its first event.
