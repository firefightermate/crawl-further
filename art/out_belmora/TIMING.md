- **BelHermit**: IdleFull / RunFull: the bell carries a load of brine (water at its lip, dripping): Bellful of Brine.
- **BelBoxer**: HopBack (4): the Footwork hop back out of reach after each punch.
- **BelDrownedBell**: IdleBare / RunBare / Punch: with the bell off his back (Bare Back). Lift starts with the bell standing in front of him (the floor bell FX is removed as it starts) and ends with the toll.
- **BelCaptain**: Grapple frames 3-7 are drawn hanging off the line; the code moves the body along the arc.
- **BelCrownThorns**: Idle/Run/Lash/Fling (5 arms), and Idle4/Run4/Lash4/Fling4 ... Idle2/Run2/Lash2/Fling2: the arm count is its shape (Regrowth). The raised arms go first: top right, top left, then the top.
- **BelMatriarch**: PlantLoop (4): held while the Riptide Lanes run (3 s), the crown glowing.
- **BelJellySiren**: Hovers 2 px. Jet is three pulses in one clip (frames 1, 5, 9 are the jets).
- **BelGulperEel**: New (replaces BelStormEel). IdleFull / RunFull: the pouch swollen pale, the tail light flickering (Full Pouch). Gag (6): an empty gulp.
- **BelKraken**: IdleOpen / RunOpen: the mantle blanched (Arms Out: three arms up, the head open).
- **BelColossus**: Idle / Run: the anemones shut into buds (dry floor). IdleOpen / RunOpen: open and waving (in brine).
- **BelChampion**: Rest (4, loop): head bowed in its corner between rounds.
- **FxBelCastBell**: Fly is drawn above the landing spot (the code plays it at the target while FxBelBellShadow grows there); Land then Stand loop until the Drowned Bell lifts it.
- **FxTentacle**: Rise (the hit, damage on frames 1-3), then the code loops Sway for the 2 s the arm stays, plays Swat at a hero within 14 px, and Sink at the end.
- Death plays once and ends on the corpse; Dead is that corpse frame. Statue = the Idle frame mapped tone-for-tone
  onto the stone ramp. Every attack has at least two wind-up frames before its first event.
