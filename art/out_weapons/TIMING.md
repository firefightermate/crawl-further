# v0.7 hero weapons: sprite timing

Generated by `tools/sprites/weapon_rigs.py`; wired up in `src/Content/WeaponPack.cs`.

## What is drawn and what is not
The hero's attack frames are baked into the vanilla hero atlas **with the weapon in hand** (e.g. AtlasHero
`1hsword_long_combo_03`): there is no separate held-weapon sprite. Every mod weapon therefore keeps the hero
frames, idle and run poses of its vanilla base weapon. Its hero clips are *renamed, re-timed copies*
(`ModW_<Id>_<n>`, angle variants `..._ang<j>`) whose AnimAttack events point at this pack's effects; vanilla
AnimMagic tags are dropped. Drawn here: the 34x41 shop bubbles and one FX sheet per weapon.

## Conventions
- Bubbles: `IconWpn<Id>Bubble`, anim `Bubble`, 2 frames x 0.5 s (second frame brightens the glow pixels).
  Diagonal grip-bottom-left / tip-top-right portrait, soft shading, no outline, like vanilla `shop_portraits_weapons_*`.
- FX: the effect ORIGIN is the CELL CENTRE, art faces right (+x), y up in game (down in the PNG).
  C# anchors these sprites `MidCenter` and sets `exSprite.offset` to the centre of the vanilla collider the effect
  rides on, so the art sits on the hitbox. Rings / patches / bursts are drawn centred on their impact point.
- Spawn points below are in pixels from the hero's FEET (x forward, y up positive as in code); the hero's attack
  node is (0, 10), so DamageEffectData offsets are added to (0, 10). Offsets rotate with the aim.
- Melee hitboxes: damage on at frame 0, off at frame 2 (0.09 to 0.11 s live, vanilla swipes are 0.117 s).

# v0.7.2: fifteen more weapons

Same conventions as above (FX origin = cell centre, art faces right, MidCenter anchor offset onto the collider centre).
Exceptions: the sling shots (FxWpnGrudgestone/Stone, FxWpnMidas/Coin) ride vanilla SlingProjectile, whose sprite is
anchored BotCenter 2 px below the origin: the pebble is drawn 6 px above the cell bottom so it sits on the ground
like vanilla's (the stone is a floor-level DamageBase shot, spawned at node + (0, -9) = 1 px above the feet).
FxWpnLash/Lash is drawn with the cell centre on the lash hitbox centre (19 px out from the hand, box 26 x 8), the tip
crack (Crack) is a separate hitbox spawned 38 px out along the aim (box 14 x 12).

# In-hand art: WpnHand_<id> sheets

`tools/sprites/weapon_hands.py` redraws every AtlasHero frame a mod weapon's clips use (idle, run, each attack clip and
its aim-angle variants): vanilla canvas (trimRect, y from the TOP: the hero's exSprite is anchored BotCenter), base
weapon pixels found by colour and erased, new weapon drawn (stick weapons: grip->tip axis by PCA of the erased pixels,
occlusion-split shafts re-joined; smears recoloured; bow/crossbow/sling recoloured with added parts). Body pixels are
byte-identical (the script refuses to write otherwise). New pixels are raw ramp colours (bin-centred RGB whose
_ColorRamp slot is pink in all four player ramps and not on/next to any hero-atlas slot), so the paletted shader draws
them as they are. Sheet = grid manifest (anim "Frames") + "hero": [[src, cell, origW, origH, x, y, w, h]] where x, y,
w, h is the element's trimRect in the vanilla canvas. WeaponPack.HeroClip swaps each AtlasHero frame for its row;
Patch_WeaponHandsMaterial keeps the renderer's own (player ramp) material, with the sheet texture, while a mod frame
shows. tools/validate.py "hands" fails if any frame a mod weapon clip uses has no row.

Vanilla smear in skin colour: the magic staff's swing arc (staff_combo_05/06) is drawn in (218, 193, 132), the same
cream as the hero's 1-2 px skin highlight. weapon_pixels() counts that colour as weapon when it forms a cluster of
5+ px or floats free of the body; those clusters go through the weapon's smear (Blightstaff green, Orrery moonlight
violet with gold sparks, Wickstaff flame). The script also refuses to write if any vanilla weapon or smear pixel is
left unchanged in any frame (check_vanilla_left), and still if any body pixel changed (check_body).

overrides the FxCommonImpactWpn default ImpactFx.Theme assigns there) and rewires each projectile's ContactData by layer:
monster (layer 14) -> `Hit`, wall (layer 9) -> `Wall`, any other inherited vanilla spawn -> none.
- `Hit` 6 fr, 0.40 s (= vanilla ContactSkeletonArcher, 6 fr 0.40 s): template is a FxKit.Visual with freeRotate,
  so the contact (spawn direction eBackwards) points its +x back along the shot like vanilla; DepthHandler -22 so a
  chest-height hit sorts in front of the victim.
- `Wall` 5 fr, 0.35 s (= vanilla ContactGenericSmall / ContactGenericWall): template is a clone of that vanilla
  contact (keeps its clink sound), MidCenter, unrotated, start angle 0, DepthHandler -4.

## Frame counts raised to vanilla
Arrows and bolts follow vanilla's 4-frame arrow (0.267 s loop, a glint running down the shaft): Arcbolt Bolt, Briarbow
Arrow, HydraBow Arrow and Shard, Heartseeker Bolt, Kunai, Wickstaff Wisp (all 4 x 0.066 s; Wisp 4 x 0.06). Loops and small
visuals: Arcbolt Arc, Gravehook Hook and Chain, Leechfang Bead, Blightstaff Spore, Reaper Soul and Frostbrand Chill are 4
frames. Briarbow Grow, Life and Wilt are 4 frames each with the same totals (0.26 / 0.42 / 0.24 s); ThornPatch damage is
now on from clip frame 4 and off at -4. HydraBow Split is 5 frames (vanilla ContactGenericSmall). Sling shots: Stone and
Coin are 8-frame tumbles (8 x 0.05 s loop). Vanilla's 16-frame SlingProjectile clip bakes the lob path into the frames
and has fewer distinct rock poses; the mod shots fly on ParticleMotion or script, so the frames carry only the spin.
